## Compilation value

This volume is a high-priority source for entity-centric magic-item extraction. Its entries provide item names/types, XP/GP values where applicable, source provenance, and descriptions. The collection intentionally aggregates items across TSR products and periodicals.

## Runtime policy

Compilation of an item from this anthology does not make the item exist in Wren's campaign and does not automatically activate optional mechanics from the item's original source.

Preserve original-source provenance on every extracted item assertion. Where an item's exact effect depends on nuanced prose, mark the assertion `source_text_required` and retain the exact locator rather than copying long prose.

## Extraction strategy

Prefer alphabetical/domain shards and stable item IDs rather than one file per item. Store aliases, item type, XP/GP values, original source, setting/scope tags, short normalized mechanics, and exact locator.
