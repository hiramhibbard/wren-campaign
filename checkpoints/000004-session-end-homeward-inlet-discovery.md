## locations_factions_world

### Coastal inlet site — newly established location
- Lies along the broader coastal route between the harbor settlement and Wren's home community; exact named geography remains unestablished.
- Wren successfully identified the inlet from the water by matching Edric's rough sketch to actual landmarks.
- A safe-enough landing exists on a narrow shingle strip farther inside the inlet where the water is calmer.
- Near the inlet mouth stand three squat, weathered, worked stone blocks, each roughly waist-high, spaced several feet apart in a rough line facing the water. They are old enough for salt/lichen/weathering to have softened their edges.
- The nearest/first stone bears deliberately carved geometric markings: repeated curving lines intersected by short straight strokes. A deeper roughly oval undercut recess sits within the design, with three short shallow grooves extending from one side.
- The first stone's recess is empty. The stone inside the recess is less weathered than the outer face. Faint scratches and a chipped edge are consistent with deliberate prying/extraction; the damage is newer than the ancient carving but not fresh enough for Wren to date more precisely than "not within the last few days."
- The second stone has the same general motif and originally retained an intact dark smooth fitted insert. Wren carefully removed it without damaging the stone or insert.
- The third stone is badly cracked/damaged; part of the corresponding recess is broken away and no intact insert remains there.
- Wren gently seated the second stone's insert in the first stone's empty recess. The shape, undercut edge, and three ridges/grooves aligned almost perfectly and the insert sat flush. No visible movement, sound, light, or other immediate reaction occurred. Wren then removed the insert and kept it.
- Wren made sketches and written notes of the site, the three stones, the carvings/recess, the prying damage, and enough surrounding landmarks to relocate the inlet.
