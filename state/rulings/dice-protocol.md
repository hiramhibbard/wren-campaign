# Dice Protocol

Materialized from the pre-sharding canonical ledger without changing campaign rules.

- Player-facing rolls: Hiram rolls physical dice for Wren and other appropriately player-facing rolls, then reports the unmodified die result when needed; the DM applies verified modifiers/rules.
- Hidden DM rolls: generate genuinely randomized programmatic results for rolls that should remain behind the screen.
- Use uniform faces and independent compound dice unless a rule specifies otherwise.
- No fudging, selective rerolls, discarded results, or narrative choice presented as a roll.
- Generate the raw result first; apply modifiers and table interpretation separately.
- Do not reveal hidden rolls merely because they were generated; reveal only perceptible information.
- If a hidden roll establishes durable campaign state, a hidden NPC/faction action, a timed development, fixed treasure/content, or another lasting fact, persist the resulting fact as DM-secret state.
- Randomness cannot silently rewrite established truth.
