# Supplement Source Routing Triggers

This is a mandatory event-driven companion to `SUPPLEMENT_SOURCE_RESOLUTION_POLICY.md`.

## General trigger
When a consequential situation clearly falls within a specialist AD&D 2e supplement domain, consult the likely specialist source family without scanning unrelated supplements.

Consultation does not equal activation.

## Race/species trigger
When an elf, dwarf, gnome, halfling, humanoid, or other covered race/species becomes consequential through NPC generation, settlement/culture creation, social interaction, history, material culture, religion, warfare, language, or race-specific institutions:
1. identify active setting/adventure scope;
2. consult the applicable race-specialist source if available;
3. use compatible guidance automatically where it does not conflict with governing setting/adventure/core material;
4. do not activate kits, optional proficiencies, alternate abilities, class limits, powers, or other mechanics without the normal activation decision.

## Class-specialist trigger
When a class-specific institution, NPC, training tradition, professional practice, kit/options question, or recurring class-domain issue becomes consequential, consult the applicable class-specialist source.

Treat descriptive/professional guidance separately from optional mechanics.

## Stronghold/castle trigger
When fortification design, castle life, construction, siege, defense, stronghold economics, garrisoning, or related procedures materially affect play, consult the applicable DM-domain guide.

## Catacomb/dungeon-specialist trigger
When tombs, catacombs, specialized subterranean construction, burial complexes, dungeon architecture, or domain-specific site procedures materially matter, consult the applicable DM-domain guide in addition to `SITE_RUNTIME_POLICY.md`.

## Arms/equipment trigger
When specialist equipment, weapon/armor detail, availability, provenance, construction, unusual gear, or repeated equipment-table lookup matters, consult the applicable equipment source. Optional or replacement mechanics remain inactive unless adopted.

## Villain trigger
When a major antagonist, villain organization, recurring adversary, long-term scheme, or villain-specific DM procedure is being created or deepened, consult the applicable villain/DM guidance source for compatible DM craft. Do not let a guide predetermine plot outcomes or override `DM_CRAFT_POLICY.md` scenario-before-story principles.

## Seafaring trigger
When ships, crews, naval movement/combat, ports, maritime hazards, shipboard operations, or sustained sea travel become consequential, consult the applicable seafaring source family plus currently governing core projections/source material.

A specialized naval subsystem that materially replaces core mechanics requires normal activation/precedence resolution; descriptive seamanship/crew/port guidance may be used when compatible.

## Setting/adventure precedence trigger
Whenever a specialist source and an active setting/adventure source both address the same subject, resolve scope before use. Specialized generic books must not flatten explicit setting/adventure treatments, and setting-specific material must not leak into unrelated generic scope.

## Performance trigger
Do not scan every supplement each turn.

Use:
`domain signal -> likely source role -> existing resolved route/cache? -> targeted lookup only if needed`

Cache source-role resolution and worthwhile deterministic projections after first use. Reopen source selection only when scope changes, precedence becomes uncertain, or a cached projection/source route is invalidated.

## Voice trigger
Preload only the specialist source rows/notes likely to matter immediately. If a consequential unpreloaded supplement rule requires exact lookup during Voice and retrieval is unavailable, preserve the pending lookup rather than guessing.
