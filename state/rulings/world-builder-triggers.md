# World Builder Runtime Triggers

Mandatory event-driven companion to `WORLD_BUILDER_RUNTIME_POLICY.md`.

## General trigger
When play/world causality requires an unresolved place, culture, government, resource pattern, settlement, route, ecology, site, history, mythology, or other world fact, use the World Builder resolver automatically rather than improvising from generic fantasy memory.

## Default microscopic trigger
For current Wren play, prefer the microscopic/local route unless a larger-scale dependency is actually implicated.

Examples:
- entering or approaching a new settlement;
- taking a previously undefined road/route;
- asking who governs or supplies a place;
- interacting with a culture/institution not yet resolved;
- exploring terrain beyond the established operational substrate;
- discovering a ruin/site whose surrounding context must now exist;
- needing local resources, trade, population, ecology, or history to adjudicate consequences.

## Settlement activation trigger
When Lowcove, Bridgeford, Saltwick, or a newly encountered settlement needs detail beyond current canon:
1. preserve all established facts;
2. identify the specific unresolved function/detail needed;
3. consult the Guidebook's local campaign area / cities-towns-villages procedures as applicable;
4. route race/class/DMGR specialist questions through supplement resolution;
5. route scenario/site needs through adventure opportunity resolution;
6. establish only the minimum durable facts required now.

Do not generate a full census, government hierarchy, street map, economy, religion, or surrounding villages unless play actually needs them.

## Geography / route trigger
When exact terrain, roads, rivers, passes, coast relationships, travel distance, or settlement placement becomes consequential:
- use established map/source/state constraints first;
- choose the smallest relevant geography procedure;
- do not retroactively force unresolved map labels into exact geography;
- preserve checkpoint 000005's explicit Home Coast geography boundary until facts are legitimately established.

## Sociology trigger
When culture/government/population/resources become materially consequential, use the sociological/kingdom procedures at the smallest needed scope.

Do not establish national politics merely to answer a village question.

## Character-based trigger
When a significant NPC/PC background, homeland, institution, family, order, or social position requires surrounding context, use the character-based approach to generate only what the character's established history now implies.

Do not bend unrelated world facts around Wren merely because he is the protagonist.

## Situation-based trigger
When an established magical phenomenon, war, migration, ecological pressure, faction struggle, supernatural condition, or other major situation requires new world structure, derive the structure from that situation's actual causal constraints.

## Historical/mythological trigger
When a current clue, ruin, institution, belief, artifact, site, or conflict depends on an unresolved past fact, establish the minimum history/mythology needed before consequences rely on it.

Do not write a complete timeline merely because one old event matters.

## Monster/ecology trigger
When world-building requires a durable creature population or ecological relationship, hand off to monster source/ecology policy before establishing the species/population.

## Site/adventure trigger
When local generation identifies an appropriate site/adventure opportunity, invoke `ADVENTURE_OPPORTUNITY_POLICY.md` automatically before deciding whether to build an original scenario.

## Random generation trigger
Use a Guidebook roll only after constraints and scope are defined. Never reroll a valid consequential result merely because another result would be more convenient or dramatic.

## Defer trigger
If a detail is not required for current causality, player decision, source reconciliation, or active-world consistency, leave it unresolved.

"Not generated yet" is not "does not exist."

## Performance trigger
Normal turn:
`cheap unresolved-world-detail check -> no material need = no Guidebook lookup`

Triggered:
`current need -> exact domain -> targeted source lookup -> bounded generation -> persist only if consequential`

## Voice trigger
If an unresolved generated fact becomes consequential during Voice and the required source is not loaded/available, preserve the pending lookup and resolve after returning to text mode rather than improvising.
