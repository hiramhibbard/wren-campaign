# Dragon Magazine Runtime Triggers

Mandatory event-driven companion to `DRAGON_MAGAZINE_SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## General trigger
When a consequential unresolved domain is one for which Dragon Magazine plausibly contains useful AD&D-era material, perform a targeted article search without waiting for Hiram to request Dragon specifically.

## Monster ecology trigger
After resolving the governing 2e monster source, if ecology/behavior/lair/social/lifecycle evidence remains materially unresolved, search for relevant Dragon ecology material before falling back to modern non-authoritative ecology inspiration.

Do not let Dragon override active monster mechanics or setting/adventure facts merely because an ecology article exists.

## Setting support trigger
When an active setting needs detail not sufficiently supplied by loaded setting sources, search Dragon for setting-specific articles before inventing broad setting canon.

Confirm edition/timeframe/scope and supersession before use.

## Religion trigger
When a deity, church, cult, specialty priesthood, ritual institution, or religious organization becomes consequential, Dragon may be searched for setting-appropriate support.

Mechanical priesthood/class options remain inactive unless explicitly adopted.

## NPC / organization trigger
When a major NPC, villain, guild, society, profession, order, patron network, or institution needs depth, search relevant Dragon article families when likely useful.

Retrieved material is candidate input, not automatic campaign truth.

## Magic trigger
When a new or unusual spell, magical tradition, item, artifact, curse, or magical phenomenon is needed, search Dragon where useful after checking more authoritative governing sources.

Do not silently grant player access or activate variant mechanics.

## Race / class trigger
When race/class culture or specialized institutions need enrichment, Dragon may supplement PHBR/setting sources where compatible.

Kits/options/mechanical variants require normal activation scope.

## DM procedure / worldbuilding trigger
When World Builder, regional runtime, site runtime, travel, economics, stronghold, environment, or another DM domain has a gap, Dragon DM-craft/procedure articles may be searched as secondary guidance.

Do not replace exact governing 2e mechanics without an explicit adoption decision.

## Adventure-component trigger
When an adventure opportunity needs a compact villain, organization, item, location idea, unusual monster treatment, magical complication, or scenario seed, Dragon may supply components.

Full adventure discovery remains routed through `ADVENTURE_OPPORTUNITY_POLICY.md`; original creation remains allowed.

## Article classification trigger
Before using retrieved Dragon material, classify its role and authority under `DRAGON_MAGAZINE_SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

At minimum determine:
- setting/generic scope;
- edition/system relevance;
- lore/guidance/mechanics/inspiration role;
- conflict/supersession status;
- whether explicit activation is needed.

## Performance trigger
Normal turn:
`cheap domain-gap check -> no Dragon relevance = no search`

Triggered:
`subject + setting/scope + role -> targeted article search -> inspect small result set -> use/reject -> cache locator if useful`

Do not scan issue runs or preload magazines.

## Voice trigger
Use cached/resolved Dragon-derived material in Voice. If an exact article fact or mechanic becomes consequential and is unavailable, preserve the pending source lookup rather than guessing.
