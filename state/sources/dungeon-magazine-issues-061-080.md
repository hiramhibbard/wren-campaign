## Selection-index discipline

- Container availability is not the same as complete adventure metadata.
- Hard filters (level/party feasibility, system/setting requirements, true geographic requirements) must come from source-stated or directly inspected data.
- Weighted tags (tone, structure, environment, adaptation cost, combat/social/exploration emphasis) may be conservative derived metadata, but must not invent scenario facts.
- Exact PDF remains authority for maps, keyed encounters, NPCs, traps, treasure, handouts, mechanics, and hidden information.
- Do not expose DM-only adventure content to the player merely because it is indexed.
