## Extraction discipline

- These bindings make exact-source finalist retrieval fast and deterministic.
- Adventure-selection metadata must remain issue-grounded. Do not infer level range, party size, setting, author, scenario structure, or special requirements from model memory.
- Container availability and adventure-record completeness are separate states.
- Conservative derived tags may be added after title/introduction inspection for location/environment, tone, structure, length, combat/social/exploration emphasis, adaptation cost, hooks, opposition, magic profile, and portability.
- Exact issue PDF remains authority for maps, keyed encounters, NPCs, traps, treasure, handouts, mechanics, boxed text, and DM-only information.
