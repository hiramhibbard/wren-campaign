## Selection-index discipline

- Exact issue binding is the availability gate; bibliographic mentions and advertisements are not substitutes.
- Adventure-level hard filters (level/party feasibility, campaign setting, class/race restrictions, geographic requirements, sequel dependencies) must come from directly inspected issue metadata.
- Weighted selector fields may include environment, tone, structure, length, combat/social/exploration emphasis, adaptation cost, hooks, opposition, magic profile, portability, and special requirements, but should be conservative derivatives of source-supported facts.
- Exact PDF remains authority for maps, keyed encounters, NPCs, traps, treasure, handouts, mechanical detail, boxed text, and DM-only content.
- Indexing an adventure does not instantiate it in Wren's world; an unused candidate remains only catalog/source metadata or a Prepared Possibility if later prepared.
