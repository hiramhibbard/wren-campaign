# DM-ONLY Home Coast World Runtime — DO NOT REVEAL TO PLAYER

This file instantiates the regional world-motion machinery defined by `REGIONAL_RUNTIME_POLICY.md` for the current Home Coast activation horizon.

It deliberately reuses already-established hidden forces instead of inventing a large new faction roster.

## Activation horizon

Current active horizon includes:
- Lowcove and immediate settled coast;
- Lowcove–Bridgeford route and Bridgeford-adjacent consequences;
- Saltwick and its near coastal/harbor approaches;
- nearby/remote Home Coast shore and coastal waters capable of affecting those settlements;
- eastern-hills/observatory direction only to the degree actors/processes there can plausibly propagate consequences toward known routes or current threads.

Do not simulate regions beyond this horizon unless a causal connection, travel plan, source timeline, or new established actor brings them into relevance.

## Active world element: coastal magical phenomenon

Type: supernatural/environmental process.

Established basis:
- old magical phenomenon interacting intermittently with coast/nearby waters;
- not created/controlled by Wren's mentor;
- capable of producing odd tides, lights, wreckage, altered animals, missing/displaced things, and related manifestations;
- eventual cause must remain internally consistent.

Current activity state: low/intermittent.

Reevaluation triggers:
- every **3 in-world days** while the Home Coast remains in the active horizon;
- immediately after a materially relevant magical/coastal event, major clue interaction, or source-defined trigger that could alter the phenomenon;
- immediately if a later established causal model requires a different schedule.

Default unresolved-outcome method while exact mechanism remains unfinalized:
- first apply any established causal constraints and recent consequences;
- if no outcome is forced, make a genuine secret 1d6 process roll:
  - 1: a minor manifestation or concrete off-screen effect occurs somewhere within current established reach;
  - 2: a subtle sign/shift occurs that may later create evidence, rumor, or environmental change but need not immediately reach Wren;
  - 3–6: no new material manifestation this interval;
- define the specific valid outcome from already established geography/process constraints before using it consequentially;
- mundane oddities must remain possible independently; do not route every unusual event to this process.

Dependencies after a material result:
- affected local encounter ingredients;
- weather/tide/visibility only if the manifestation actually changes them;
- local rumor/community-pressure state if observable;
- mentor/outside-seeker information if they plausibly detect it;
- clues/threads and Established DM Truth where necessary.

## Active world element: mentor's private inquiry

Type: intelligent actor/project.

Established goal: understand and potentially exploit the coastal phenomenon before rivals while withholding some information from Wren.

Current reach: Home Coast plus plausible contacts/resources; exact network remains unestablished.

Reevaluation triggers:
- every **3 in-world days**;
- immediately when Wren reports a meaningful clue, materially changes their relationship, exposes new information, or affects a site/object she is tracking;
- immediately when evidence of rival activity becomes available to her.

Resolution:
- determine actions from established goals, knowledge, risk, resources, and relationship state;
- if multiple actions remain comparably plausible, define the bounded options first and use a genuine secret roll;
- checkpoint significant discoveries/actions/relationship consequences;
- do not manufacture an action solely to redirect Wren toward the main mystery.

Potential regional dependencies:
- NPC movements/contact attempts;
- rumor/information flow;
- investigation of sites/objects;
- outside-seeker awareness;
- encounter eligibility only if her actions create an actual presence/event.

## Active world element: outside seekers

Type: intelligent outside actor/faction placeholder.

Established basis:
- at least one intelligent outside party searches for related old knowledge;
- initially not aware of Wren personally;
- capable of negotiation or conflict;
- exact identity/institutional form remains unfixed until setting/source details support it.

Current activity state: active but distant.

Reevaluation triggers:
- every **7 in-world days** while distant from the Home Coast activation horizon;
- switch to every **3 in-world days** once established actions/place them within practical influence of Lowcove, Bridgeford, Saltwick, or the active coastal mystery area;
- immediate reevaluation if they obtain a major clue, lose a resource, encounter mentor activity, or become aware of Wren.

Resolution:
- advance from goals, known information, resources, geography, and opposition;
- do not establish exact identity merely to fill a clock field;
- before a clue/encounter depends on their concrete identity or method, resolve the minimum required hidden truth and persist it;
- leave evidence before direct contact when established actions naturally create it.

Potential regional dependencies:
- clues/tracks/rumors;
- special encounter entries only after actual local presence is established;
- faction awareness and territory/reach;
- competition over sites/objects.

## Active world element: local community pressures

Type: social/economic/rumor process, not a unified faction.

Established basis:
- villagers/fishers possess incomplete observations and folklore;
- fear, superstition, practical economic concerns, and personal loyalties can shape responses;
- the whole community is not a conspiracy.

Reevaluation triggers:
- every **7 in-world days** while significant unusual events/economic pressures remain active;
- immediately after a public manifestation, disappearance, wreck, major accident, violent incident, alarming rumor, market disruption, or other event likely to spread socially.

Resolution:
- determine who plausibly observed what and through which channels information spreads;
- use `state/rulings/knowledge-reliability-and-rumors.md` for consequential reports/beliefs;
- generate disagreement and uneven knowledge rather than one village-wide belief;
- economic/social consequences should follow actual events, not arbitrary mood shifts.

Potential regional dependencies:
- rumor pools;
- NPC attitudes;
- traffic/trade patterns;
- local authority/community responses once those institutions are established;
- encounter ingredients in settled areas.

## Environmental process: ordinary Home Coast weather

Type: regional environmental state.

Climate basis: temperate maritime/coastal per `state/locations/home-coast/runtime-profile.md`.

Weather generation/reevaluation:
- when meaningful sea travel occurs, determine each relevant day's conditions using the active core DMG ship-weather projection unless a more specific explicitly active source governs;
- during consequential overland/coastal travel, establish weather for the travel interval consistent with climate/season and preserve it long enough to affect visibility, movement, navigation, exposure, encounters, and scene continuity;
- ordinary indoor/local scenes do not require rolling daily weather merely to populate a log;
- if multiple in-world days pass off-screen and weather has no downstream consequence, weather may remain abstract rather than generating unused daily records;
- any storm/fog/strong-wind state that creates continuing consequences must carry its next reevaluation/ending trigger.

## Population/ecology runtime

No additional specific hostile population/faction (for example goblins, bandits, wolves, pirates) is established by this file merely to populate encounters.

This is **not** a canonical assertion that such populations do not exist. Their detailed presence remains unestablished until source, regional activation, a valid encounter-generation process, clue, or other causal need determines it.

When a population capable of independent movement/pressure becomes consequential:
1. establish its current plausible location/reach and source/ecological basis;
2. decide whether it is latent or active;
3. if active, assign an explicit reevaluation trigger/cadence before relying on future DM recollection;
4. connect it to affected encounter profiles/rumors/economy/factions;
5. persist movement/state changes once consequential.

## World-motion propagation rule

When an active element changes state:
1. identify only dependent regional artifacts/entities;
2. invalidate/refresh affected encounter ingredients/table weights, rumors, routes, weather assumptions, faction awareness, or other state;
3. do not rewrite unrelated regions;
4. schedule the next trigger for the changed element if still active;
5. checkpoint significant durable hidden changes at the next normal save point.

## Combat fairness

The runtime must never create or strengthen a hostile actor merely because Wren has not fought recently. Combat opportunities arise from actual keyed encounters, valid random encounters, reactions, active-world consequences, adventures, or Wren's choices.

Likewise, do not suppress a legitimately triggered dangerous result merely because Wren is low level. Use source/world logic, reaction, surprise, morale, distance, avoidance, and consequences normally; no plot protection.
