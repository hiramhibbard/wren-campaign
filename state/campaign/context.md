# Campaign Context and Setting Constraints

Materialized from the pre-sharding canonical ledger without changing campaign facts except where explicitly updated by Hiram.

## Campaign framing
- Long-term solo AD&D 2nd Edition campaign.
- Preserve fragile low-level play, meaningful danger, and gradual earned growth in power.
- Hiram prefers discovering the world and adventures through play rather than inventing much of them himself.
- Campaign may begin in a remote area and can eventually develop a stronger home-base feeling through play.

## Setting status
- Mystara has been strongly considered as the starting setting but is **not locked** unless established in play.
- Ravenloft has been discussed only as a possible future direction and is **not locked**.
- Exact named campaign setting, institutions, cosmology, political geography, demography, racial homelands, and ancient history remain unestablished unless later established from play/source material.
- Settlement names Lowcove, Bridgeford, and Saltwick are established local campaign names, but they do not by themselves lock a published campaign setting.

## Peoples / race-species knowledge
- Wren's immediate home coast is predominantly human in ordinary day-to-day life; this is a local demographic tendency, not evidence that the wider world is human-only.
- Humans are **not** the only people in the wider world. Other AD&D peoples/races exist and are ordinary known facts of the world to Wren.
- Wren already knows that elves, dwarves, halflings, gnomes, half-elves, and other setting-appropriate peoples exist. Encountering one is not inherently a shocking revelation merely because his immediate coastal corner has been mostly human.
- Do not retroactively classify every previously encountered NPC as human solely because race/species was not described. Preserve any established ancestry; where ancestry is genuinely unset, determine it only when relevant and consistently with source/setting/local context.
- Do not establish worldwide population percentages, specific racial homelands, political relations, or setting-specific prevalence until the governing campaign setting/source or play establishes them.

These are campaign constraints and player-known world facts, not hidden world truths. Exact setting canon remains governed by Hiram's uploaded source material once a setting is established.
