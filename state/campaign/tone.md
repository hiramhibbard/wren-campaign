# Wren Campaign Tone Profile

This file stores Hiram's current campaign tone and presentation preferences. It is intentionally easy to change at any time.

## Authority and scope

- This is a **mutable presentation/preference layer**, not hidden world truth and not a retroactive rewrite of campaign events.
- Tone affects narration, scene framing, descriptive emphasis, dialogue style, humor, horror intensity, wonder, pacing, and how strongly certain aesthetic qualities are foregrounded.
- Tone does **not** override AD&D 2e mechanics, source canon, established facts, NPC motives, secret DM truth, fair consequences, random outcomes, danger, failure, or mortality.
- A tone change takes effect prospectively unless Hiram explicitly asks to reinterpret prior presentation. It does not alter what actually happened.
- Hiram may change this profile with ordinary language at any time, e.g. "make it eerier," "less grim," "more adventurous," or "dial back the humor." Such requests should update this file at the next safe canonical write point.

## Current tonal anchors

- Grounded fantasy rather than constant spectacle.
- Strong sense of place, weather, travel, ordinary work, material constraints, and local life.
- Wonder and the unknown should feel meaningful because they emerge from a believable world.
- Fragile low-level play, meaningful danger, and gradual earned growth remain central.
- Discovery should come through play rather than exposition dumps or handing Wren answers prematurely.
- The world should feel larger than Wren's current knowledge and should contain genuine mystery, hidden causes, and things he may never discover.
- NPCs should feel like people with their own motives, habits, limitations, obligations, and lives rather than quest dispensers.
- Consequences should be real. Wren has no plot armor.

## Current presentation balance

These are soft defaults, not numeric rules:

- **Grounded ↔ fantastical:** grounded with room for striking fantasy when earned.
- **Hopeful ↔ bleak:** serious and sometimes harsh, but not relentlessly bleak.
- **Light ↔ grim:** mature and dangerous without defaulting to grimdark.
- **Restrained ↔ pulpy:** restrained; avoid constant cinematic escalation.
- **Subtle ↔ overtly weird:** subtle-to-moderate; strange elements should often accumulate meaning before becoming fully understood.
- **Serious ↔ humorous:** mostly serious/naturalistic, with organic humor from people and situations rather than frequent jokes.
- **Cozy/home-base ↔ relentless pressure:** allow warmth, family, rest, ordinary life, and eventual home-base attachment alongside adventure and danger.

## Style guidance

- Favor concrete sensory detail and actionable information over florid prose.
- Keep narration concise enough for table play; expand when a scene genuinely benefits from atmosphere or emotional weight.
- Avoid making every moment ominous, every NPC cryptic, or every mundane object secretly important.
- Avoid modern quippy sameness in NPC dialogue.
- Let silence, uncertainty, routine, beauty, danger, humor, and dread coexist naturally.
- Preserve Hiram's control of Wren's choices, speech, intentions, and emotional commitments.
