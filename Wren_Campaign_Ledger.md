## DM Operating Protocol --- Read Before Running or Continuing Play

These rules are part of the campaign record and are mandatory workflow
for future sessions.

1.  **Retrieve before recall.** Before answering any campaign-state
    question whose exact value matters, retrieve the relevant canonical
    campaign record. Do not rely on conversational memory for exact
    values.
2.  **Never invent established values.** If an exact value is not
    established in the ledger or an authoritative source, say it is
    unknown/unestablished rather than filling it in.
3.  **Campaign state beats recollection.** When recollection conflicts
    with the canonical ledger, use the ledger unless there is evidence
    that the ledger itself is stale or erroneous; if so, stop and
    reconcile deliberately.
4.  **Checkpoint after meaningful state changes.** Persist durable
    changes to character state, inventory, money, spells, XP, injuries,
    location, chronology, NPC state, clues, rulings, and DM-only truths
    at appropriate safe boundaries.
5.  **Checkpoint before transitions.** Before ending a session or
    making a transition that risks losing state, persist all pending
    changes and verify the write.
6.  **Preserve uncertainty.** Rumors, suspicions, unresolved costs,
    inferred facts, and hidden information must remain explicitly
    uncertain until established in play or by an authoritative source.
7.  **Separate player knowledge from DM truth.** Do not leak DM-only
    truths into player-facing narration or summaries.
8.  **Use actual AD&D 2e mechanics.** Apply the rules when uncertainty
    matters; use secret DM rolls where appropriate and do not fudge
    outcomes.
9.  **Gameplay first.** Run the campaign as a game world with player
    agency, not as collaborative novel-writing. The player controls
    Wren; the DM controls the world, NPCs, opposition, hidden
    information, and consequences.
10. **Persistent world.** Maintain prepared locations, motives,
    factions, dangers, secrets, clues, timelines, and likely
    developments. Improvisation must remain consistent with established
    world state.
11. **Published AD&D material may be real campaign material.** Draw
    freely from uploaded AD&D 2e resources, adventures, and Dragon/
    Dungeon material when they fit naturally; preserve substantive
    scenario content when used.
12. **Record consequential rulings.** Preserve campaign-specific rules
    interpretations and optional-rule choices for future consistency.
13. **Audit periodically.** After every three completed play sessions,
    and at major arc/phase boundaries, reconcile current state against
    recent checkpoints.
14. **Maintain a Current Resume Packet.** Keep a compact resume packet
    containing only what is needed to resume play quickly, and refresh
    it at every checkpoint.
15. **Post-write readback verification.** After every persistent ledger
    write, retrieve enough of the newly stored canonical record to
    verify that the intended checkpoint/change and critical updated
    values are actually present. A successful write alone is not
    sufficient verification.
16. **Checkpoint trigger precedence.** Once a checkpoint trigger fires,
    persistence work is part of the current task and must be completed
    at the next safe/natural boundary before unrelated campaign work
    continues.
17. **Resume packet disagreement rule.** If the Current Resume Packet
    conflicts with a detailed canonical section, stop and reconcile
    using the most recent verified checkpoint/change history.
18. **Session-end sign-off barrier.** When the player indicates that a
    play session is ending, perform the session-ending checkpoint,
    refresh the Current Resume Packet, persist all pending changes, and
    complete post-write readback verification before giving final
    campaign sign-off. If persistence cannot be completed, explicitly
    say the checkpoint is not yet safely saved.
19. **Canonical-ledger path barrier.** For normal campaign retrieval,
    updates, checkpoints, and resume-state reads, use only
    `/Wren Campaign/Wren_Campaign_Ledger.md` as the canonical ledger.
    Files in `/Wren Campaign/Backups/` or files whose names contain
    backup/archive/checkpoint markers are recovery-only and must never
    silently replace current state.
20. **Fact domains.** Maintain distinct character/mechanical state,
    chronology/session state, NPC state, active threads/clues/rumors,
    campaign rulings, and DM-only truths.
